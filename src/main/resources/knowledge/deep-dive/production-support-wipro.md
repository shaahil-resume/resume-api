At Wipro, Shaahil was part of the platform SWAT team and led the resolution of critical production incidents on the Core Benefits Administration platform. The problems he fixed included slow queries, plans not showing, the wrong plan showing, and users being unable to add plans. In one case, a bug fix caused a wrong calculation that showed incorrect plan information to a large group of people, and he fixed it. He reduced the backlog every week, and received multiple organisational awards for this work. Incident closure time fell by 40% and the aging ticket backlog fell by 30% within 3 months, while the platform sustained 99.9% stability. He introduced Prometheus and Grafana observability, which gave visibility into memory and resource usage across the application platform.
